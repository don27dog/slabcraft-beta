How to measure centering with the lines
  1. Orange dashed lines = the card's edges. Drag each one onto the spot where the card stops and the table or background starts.
  2. Green solid lines = the printed border. Drag each one onto the inside edge of the border, where the border color meets the picture or design.
  3. The gap between orange and green is that side's border. The app compares opposite sides: left against right, and top against bottom.
  4. Read the numbers. 50/50 is perfect. In the example, the left border is about twice the right, which reads about 65/35.

What you need

Penny sleevesSoft, acid-free, PVC-free. The card's first layer.
Blank display slabsSnap-together acrylic cases with a label window, sold unbranded. Match the inner well to card thickness.
Or magnetic holdersOne-touch style. 35pt fits most base cards; thick relics need 55pt to 180pt.
Label stockGlossy photo paper or 65–110 lb cardstock. Sticker sheets work for magnetic holders.
Tamper sealsHolographic “void” stickers that tear if the case is opened. Write the cert # on them.
Prep kitNitrile gloves, microfiber cloth, rubber blower bulb, paper trimmer or ruler and craft knife.

Card thickness quick guide

  • 20ptMost base cards from the 1980s to today
  • 35ptThicker base and many inserts
  • 55–75ptMemorabilia and patch cards
  • 100pt+Thick relics and booklet cards

Steps

  1. Grade before you sealPhotograph and grade the card bare. Once it is sealed, glare and plastic hide flaws.
  2. Print and trim the labelDownload from Label Studio, print at actual size, trim on the outer border line.
  3. Prep the cardGloves on. Puff away dust with the blower. Never wipe the card surface.
  4. Sleeve itSlide the card into a penny sleeve top-first so the corners don't catch.
  5. Load the slabSeat the sleeved card in the well. Drop the label in the top window, face out.
  6. Close and sealSnap the case shut, then put a tamper seal across the seam. Write the cert # and date on it.
  7. Log and storeSave the card in My Vault. Store slabs upright, out of direct sun, in a cool, dry spot.
